# Pose library: how it's built

`site/data/poses.json` is generated by `tools/build_library.py` from **3DYoga90** (Kim et al., 2023). 3DYoga90 holds MediaPipe BlazePose 3D world landmarks extracted from YouTube yoga videos, and its annotations are CC BY-NC 4.0. That's fine for this non-commercial project; any derived data we publish must keep the attribution and the non-commercial terms.

We used up to 20 random held sequences per pose (all of them for 5 poses that needed more), 2,166 sequences in total.

## Method (per pose)
1. **Held skeleton:** the median of the steadiest half of each sequence's frames, scaled so torso length = 1.
2. **Label sanity check:** sequences that contradict their category are dropped. Examples: a "forward bend" whose torso is upright (the standing phase before the fold), or an "inversion" whose head is above the hips.
3. **Camera view:** each sequence is classed as front, side or lying (from the chest direction in 3D). The pose uses its majority view, because instructors film each pose from the angle that shows it best. Lying poses are always shown side-on.
4. **Canonicalise:** rotate every sequence about the vertical axis into that view. Then flip it to the same working side (reflect across the body's midline), choosing whichever side is closest to the group.
5. **Target:** the circular median, across people, of 9 image-plane segment angles: torso, both upper arms, forearms, thighs and shins. These are the same features the game measures (`site/js/angles.js`).
6. **Tolerance and weight:**
   - Tolerance = max(base, 0.75 × the 75th percentile of people's deviation). Base: torso 12°, upper arm 15°, forearm 20°, thigh 12°, shin 15°.
   - Weight = base ÷ (1 + spread/30). Base: torso 1.5, forearm 0.5, others 1.0.
   - Where real people vary a lot, scoring is more lenient and that segment counts for less.
7. **Legibility:** a pose is dropped when people disagree too much on its 2D shape from one camera (mean spread > 35°) or when fewer than 5 sequences hold still.
8. **Figure shown to players:** the *medoid*, the real person whose pose is closest to the target. Real people performed it, so it's achievable.

## Difficulty (1–10)
`raw = 0.40·balance + 0.25·flexibility + 0.25·strength + 0.10·coordination`, `difficulty = 1 + 9·clip((raw − 0.05)/0.75)`.

- **Balance:** from the 3DYoga90 hierarchy (arm balances 1.0, inversions 0.9, side plank 0.5) or, for standing poses, the skeleton (one foot clearly off the floor 0.75; with a hand down 0.5; feet together 0.25; wide stance 0.1). Seated and lying poses get 0.
- **Flexibility:** the fraction of normal adult range of motion used at the two most demanding joints, plus a spine prior per category. ROM values (hip flexion 120°, knee flexion 135°, shoulder flexion 180°, hip abduction 45°) are from AAOS, Greene & Heckman (1994), *The Clinical Measurement of Joint Motion*.
- **Strength:** a category prior (planks, arm balances, inversions), a weight-bearing knee bend in standing poses, and arms held out at 60–120°.
- **Coordination:** left/right asymmetry of the 3D elbow, shoulder, hip and knee angles.

Distribution over the 70 poses (by integer bucket): 1: 2 · 2: 9 · 3: 11 · 4: 13 · 5: 15 · 6: 3 · 7: 4 · 8: 2 · 9: 4 · 10: 7. 12 poses are flagged **advanced** (all inversions and arm balances, plus anything at 8.5 or above) and carry an on-screen warning.

**Known weaknesses:** big backbends are probably rated too easy (Upward Bow 5.5, Camel 4.6), and Side Reclining Leg Lift too hard (8.4). The weights and priors are hand-set and provisional.

## Coverage
- **Kept 70:** 17 standing, 15 sitting, 16 reclining, 10 wheel/backbend, 5 inverted, 7 arm balances. 17 front-view, 53 side-view.
- **Dropped 20** (people disagree too much from one camera, or too few steady clips): Goddess, Half Moon, Gate, Warrior 1, Low Lunge, High Lunge, Lord of the Dance, Standing Big Toe Hold, Cow Face, Revolved Head to Knee, Shooting Bow, Cockerel, Flying Splits, Plow, Happy Baby, Supine Twist, Frog, Child, Lizard, Wild Things.
- Several of these (Warrior 1, the lunges) are probably fixable with better side alignment for poses filmed from mixed angles.

## Not done yet (from the brief)
- **Modifiers** (arm variants, depth variants, gaze/rotation) are not implemented. As a result, the schedule rule "no identical pose+modifier within a year" can't be met; it's a `todo` test.
- Poses are not yet checked against our *own* recordings. A first check against real YogNet videos is in [build-status.md](build-status.md).
